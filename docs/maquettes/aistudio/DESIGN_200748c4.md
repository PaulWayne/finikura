## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a gentle, nostalgic, and premium editorial identity tailored for a refined children's and lifestyle brand. It balances classic warmth, modern European restraint, and tactile softness. The target audience comprises discerning parents, gift buyers, and caregivers who value artisanal quality, mindful living, and gentle aesthetics over loud commercial noise.

The overall design style synthesizes **Minimalism** with **Editorial Softness**:
- Generous, serene whitespace anchored by warm neutral tones.
- Meticulously typeset display typography evoking storybooks and high-end publications.
- Pure avoidance of harsh contrasts, pure blacks, or industrial neons.
- Imagery guidelines are treated as core interface tokens: soft studio compositions set against warm beige surfaces, flooded with natural ambient light, free from clutter or harsh directional shadows.

## Colors

The palette is strictly curated around muted natural earth pigments, mineral washes, and warm parchment surfaces.

### Functional Roles & Application Rules
- **Canvas Base (`#FDF7F2`)**: The universal page background. Delivers a soft, non-glare reading environment.
- **Surface Alternates & Containers (`#EADCCC`)**: Reserved for alternating narrative blocks, card backings, banner backdrops, and the "À propos" brand statement panels.
- **Primary Ink & Lines (`#5E5245`)**: Used for all editorial headings, body paragraphs, navigation links, and delicate hairline icons (stroke weight: 1.25px to 1.5px). Never substitute with #000000.
- **Brand Interactive Primary (`#8A7058`)**: Solid taupe used for primary CTAs, key focus rings, and active state indicators.
- **Announcement & Calming Accent (`#9EA593`)**: Sage green allocated strictly to top announcement tickers, highlight pills, and informational notifications. Pair with `#FDF7F2` or `#5E5245` typography.
- **Subtle Accents (`#CA7154` Terracotta, `#97AFC4` Light Blue)**: Used with extreme discretion for small tags, seasonal micro-badges, or subtle status dots. They must never dominate container backgrounds.
- **Restricted Logo Exceptions**: Deep navy (`#304054`) and dusty rose (`#C8807C`) are strictly quarantined to the static SVG vector logo asset and may not be extracted into UI components, borders, or typography.

## Typography

The type system blends the literary grace of **Playfair Display** with the clean geometric legibility of a modern sans-serif body (*Work Sans*).

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Headings**: Always set in Playfair Display with color `#5E5245`. Italics may be utilized selectively for romantic single-word accents in editorial banners.
- **Section Eyebrows / Section Labels**: Must be uppercase, tracked widely (`letterSpacing: 0.15em`), and set at a small scale (11–13px) to produce an archival catalog feel.
- **Body Minimum**: Regular paragraph copy must never drop below 16px to protect readability and optical comfort against the warm canvas.
- **Color Discipline**: All typographic hierarchy levels inherit `#5E5245` as default text, muted body uses `#8A7058`, and inverted elements (on primary buttons) use `#FDF7F2` or pure white.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes expansive breathing room, rhythm, and clear vertical pacing.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored within a maximum width of `1280px`. Section padding transitions gracefully using `space-xl` and higher multiples (`4rem` to `6rem`) to cultivate unhurried luxury.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 2rem side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with strict 1.25rem outer gutters. Horizontal card rails with edge peeking are preferred over dense stacked cards for product grids.
- **Content Blocks**: Structural alternates ("À propos" bands, featured collections) span the full viewport width with `#EADCCC`, while internal copy conforms to the aligned grid columns.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally rejects heavy drop shadows, synthetic blurs, or plastic skeuomorphism. Visual hierarchy is established strictly via:

- **Tonal Contrast**: Stacking `#EADCCC` cards onto the `#FDF7F2` canvas creates natural depth without drop shadows.
- **Hairline Framing**: Fine borders tinted to `#5E5245` with an opacity of 12% (`rgba(94, 82, 69, 0.12)`) establish division without clutter.
- **Ambient Floor Shadow**: If a floating navigation header or overlay modal requires separation, apply a whisper-soft diffused shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(94, 82, 69, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses gentle, tailored corners (`rounded-sm` to `rounded-md`). Pill shapes and circular components are restrained, reserved exclusively for micro-tags or floating status chips.

- Interactive buttons and inputs: `rounded-sm` (4px) to `rounded-md` (6px) to maintain a grounded architectural quality.
- Imagery containers: Matching 4px–6px corner radius. No circular profile badges or pill image masks; photos remain classic rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid background `#8A7058`, high-contrast text (`#FFFFFF` or `#FDF7F2`). Minimum height: `44px` (touch-target compliance). Border radius: `4px` to `6px`. Hover state: Lightens softly via a subtle white overlay (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) or shifts background to `#79614B`.
- **Secondary**: Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#8A7058`, text in `#8A7058`. Hover: Subtle fill with `#EADCCC` at 40% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Link**: Underlined text in `#5E5245` with a 1px offset underline.

### Announcement Bar
- Background: `#9EA593`.
- Typography: `#FDF7F2` or `#5E5245`, 13px medium weight, centered with balanced letter spacing.
- Height: Compact, 36px to 40px.

### Cards & Narrative Panels
- Container: Background `#EADCCC`.
- Borders: None or 1px hairline `rgba(94, 82, 69, 0.08)`.
- Padding: `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- Structure: Image occupies full top radius or sits inset with equal `1rem` margins.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- Base Input: Background `#FDF7F2`, 1.5px border `#EADCCC`. Focused state switches border to `#8A7058`.
- Height: `44px` minimum. Placeholder text: `#5E5245` at 50% opacity.
- Checkboxes/Radios: `#8A7058` active fill, `#FDF7F2` checkmark icon.

### Chips & Badges
- Backgrounds: Either `#CA7154` (terracotta) or `#97AFC4` (soft blue) used with 15% opacity fills and 100% solid text of the same hue, or solid fill with `#FDF7F2` text for sale/limited callouts.
- Typography: Upper-case, 11px, bold.

### Imagery Framing
- All photographs must feature warm beige backgrounds, soft non-directional studio light, and children styled strictly in earthy sage, cream, terracotta, beige, and dusty blue garments.
- Never place dark gradients or black drop-offs over photography.
